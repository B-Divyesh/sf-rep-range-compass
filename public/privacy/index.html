Your training data stays in your browser

Effective 6 September 2026. Rep Range Compass works without an account. It stores your exercise settings, unfinished session, and completed set history in IndexedDB in this browser.

What leaves your device

Training history, exercise names, weights, reps, and RIR stay in your browser. CSV import and export run on your device. The app has no analytics, ad pixels, remote fonts, or tracking scripts.

If you buy or restore Compass Plus, Sociobot/Dodo handles checkout. The app sends only a license token to the Sociobot verification endpoint. It stores that token and a cached verification result in localStorage. The app never receives card details.

Your control

Use Export CSV to make a copy. Clear all local data deletes the training data stored by this app in this browser. Browser site-data settings can also remove the data and your saved license token.
