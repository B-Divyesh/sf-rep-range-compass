Privacy stays on your side.

Effective 28 August 2026. Rep Range Compass is designed to work without an account. Your exercise settings, unfinished session, and completed set history are stored in IndexedDB in your browser.

What leaves your device

No training history, exercise name, weight, reps, or RIR is sent to us. There are no analytics, ad pixels, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts. CSV import and export happen entirely on your device.

If you buy or restore Compass Plus, checkout occurs on Sociobot/Dodo and the license token is sent to the Sociobot verification endpoint. The token and a cached verification result are stored in localStorage. The billing service may process your email, payment details, IP address, and transaction records under its own legal obligations; this app never receives your card details.

Your control

Use “Export CSV” to make a portable copy. “Clear all local data” deletes the training data stored by this app in the current browser. You can also clear the app’s site data in browser settings. Removing browser data may remove your saved license token; retain your receipt to restore it.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through Sociobot. This policy will be updated here if the app’s data practices change.
